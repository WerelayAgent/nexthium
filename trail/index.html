A wallet, a token or a deployer. Nexthium loads where its money came from and where it went.

Start from any address. Each node you click opens the next hop, so you follow the money one step at a time.

Reading the graph

  • Circles are wallets, squares are contracts, the dashed ring is mint and burn.
  • Thicker lines carry more transfers. Arrows point where the money went.
  • Red marks a pattern worth a closer look.
