Approval

Every draft is checked twice. Then you sign.

Nexthium runs each transaction against current chain state, lists the balance changes in plain words and flags anything unusual. The approval page runs it again right before your wallet opens. Your keys stay in your wallet the whole time.

  • Dry runTested on current chain state.
  • Plain wordsEach balance change spelled out.
  • FlagsApprovals, unknown contracts, reverts.
  • Your walletThe one place a signature happens.

Swaps

Trade any token by name. One signature from ETH.

Ask for a quote with a symbol or a contract address. Nexthium routes through Uniswap v4, directly or through ETH or USDG, and takes its 0.5% from the output inside the same transaction.

Contracts

Describe a token. Own the contract.

Pick an OpenZeppelin-based template, compile it, see the gas estimate, deploy it from your wallet and publish its source on the explorer. The contract belongs to the wallet that deploys it.
